= Applied Systems
:toc:

A home for applying mathematical ideas to decisions, organisations and designed
systems. Modelling is one activity here, alongside measurement, intervention
design, implementation and evaluation. Type theory is one source of useful
ideas; the scope is not restricted to it or to business.

This area contains reusable methods, research designs and explicitly synthetic
examples. It is readable source material with no additional file encryption.
Organisation-specific papers, accounts, correspondence, personal information and
working datasets belong in their own appropriately protected repositories.
Do not copy a private case here merely by changing its name.

== How a project grows here

. State the decision, affected people, commitments and constraints.
. Separate observations, assumptions, estimates and unanswered questions.
. Specify a small model, its units, dependencies and failure conditions.
. Use a synthetic example before connecting any protected real-world case.
. Compare interventions, including timing, workload and distribution of effects.
. Record what was tested and what remains unproven.

The initial management-accounting material is a proposed design, not an adopted
accounting standard or a validated causal theory. Accounting recognition rules
remain with the applicable reporting framework. Proofs and implementations with
an established home remain there; Mathesis links to their evidence rather than
duplicating it or upgrading its assurance status.

Before public reuse, review text, filenames, figures, document metadata and Git
history for case-specific information and third-party rights. Public examples
must be synthetic by construction or separately cleared for publication.
