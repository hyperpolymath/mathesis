= Accountability-based management accounting
:toc:

Status: exploratory design, 2026-11-07. This is a management-accounting research
programme, not a claim of compliance with a financial-reporting standard.

== Decision purpose

Connect a commitment to the resources used, the people affected, the intended
benefit, possible harm and the evidence for what actually happened. Support
decisions that remove avoidable harm and improve useful outcomes without
concealing unmet obligations or transferring costs to unpaid labour.

The core record is a decision or intervention linked to events and accounting
entries. A fine-grained transaction is useful evidence, but finer allocation
does not make a cost estimate causally exact.

== Distinct views

. Financial accounting: preserve ledger entries and the applicable recognition,
  measurement and disclosure rules. Reconcile management totals to the ledger.
. Resource use: transactions, activities, operational drivers, supplied capacity
  and unused capacity, with allocation rules made explicit.
. Incremental decisions: additional cash receipts, cash costs, opportunity costs
  and constraints relative to a defined counterfactual.
. Full costs: direct costs, support capacity and attributed common costs; explain
  the allocation basis and avoid treating an allocation as an avoidable saving.
. Accountability: commitments, service outcomes, affected groups, owners,
  evidence quality, review dates and unresolved harm.

These are views of linked evidence, not amounts to add together indiscriminately.
Time-driven or activity-based costs, driver-based planning and bottleneck
analysis answer different questions. Any transaction-level costing approach
must expose the assumptions behind distributed overhead.

== Minimal mathematical design

Let x describe an intervention and s a scenario. Let C_t(x,s) be cash at period
t, H_t(x,s) required support hours and K_t(s) available support hours. Let O(x,s)
be a vector of service and mission outcomes rather than an unexplained money
score. Compare feasible interventions under explicit priorities, for example:

[source,text]
----
C_t = C_(t-1) + cash receipts_t - cash payments_t
C_t >= agreed cash floor_t
H_t <= K_t
service outcomes >= agreed service floors
entitlements and implementation constraints are satisfied
----

Missing opening cash makes the cash feasibility verdict unknown. An attractive
annual result cannot override an unfunded payment date or unavailable staff.
Do not invent shadow prices for rights, dignity or service quality. Where
trade-offs cannot be legitimately combined, show the separate objectives and
the feasible alternatives.

== Mechanisms, uncertainty and learning

The link:event-map.mmd[event map] is a proposed causal structure. Each arrow needs
evidence, a lag and possible confounders. Feedback can be helpful or harmful:
growth can fund support, while excessive workload can impair service and future
retention. Model both before treating recruitment as unconstrained income.

Use uncertainty analysis to identify which missing observations could change a
decision. Measure those first when the expected value of better information
exceeds the measurement cost. Expected-value calculations require defensible
probabilities; otherwise report scenario bounds and unresolved uncertainty.

Validation should distinguish arithmetic checks, reconciliation, empirical
calibration and formal proofs. Passing one does not establish the others.
