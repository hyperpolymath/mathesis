= Decision record template

Status: unfilled template. Blank means unanswered.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Field |Working entry
|Decision and accountable owner |
|Decision deadline and review date |
|People affected and consultation needed |
|Commitments and service floors |
|Current problem, with source and evidence status |
|Options, including the baseline |
|Expected benefits and possible harms, by affected group |
|Cash timing and minimum cash balance |
|Incremental costs, full costs and allocation basis |
|Required workload and available capacity |
|Key dependencies, feedback and time lags |
|Legal, employment, governance and cultural constraints to verify |
|Assumptions, missing inputs and most valuable next measurement |
|Stop, adapt and scale conditions |
|Decision, reasons, dissent and authorisation |
|Observed results and next review |
|===

Link each important assertion to its source or label it as an estimate,
illustration or unresolved claim. Do not record an unverified entitlement
interpretation as a settled rule, or a budget assumption as an observed saving.
