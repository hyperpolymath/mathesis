= Liquidity and short-horizon forecasting

A rolling 13-week forecast needs reconciled opening available cash, restrictions
on funds, dated receipts and payments, commitments, arrears, available facilities
and an approved minimum cash floor. Historical accounts cannot substitute for
current bank balances or payment dates.

[source,text]
----
closing_cash[w] = opening_cash[w] + receipts[w] - payments[w]
opening_cash[w+1] = closing_cash[w]
gap = max(0, max_w(cash_floor[w] - closing_cash[w]))
----

Count borrowing only when available on the needed date and record its repayment
and cost. Separate restricted money from amounts usable for ordinary payments.
Leave missing amounts blank and display incomplete coverage; do not calculate a
definitive minimum balance from a partially populated schedule.

Keep the observed/current-input forecast separate from illustrative stress
tests. Test receipt delays, collection losses, gaps in rental income and benefit
uptake jointly where dependencies justify it. Distinguish an early-warning
threshold, a breach of an agreed buffer and inability to meet a payment.

Any longer-term initiative must show its first-quarter funding requirement
alongside its later payoff. Reconcile forecast to actual cash weekly and record
whether variances reflect amount, timing, missing commitments or model error.
