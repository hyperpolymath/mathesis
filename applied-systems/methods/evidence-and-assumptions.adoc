= Evidence and assumptions

Every material input needs a description, unit, period, source, owner, evidence
status, last verification date and dependencies. Use independent fields for
evidence quality and access classification: public does not mean verified;
private does not mean true.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Evidence status |Meaning
|Verified observation |Checked against an identified source for a stated date and definition.
|Reported |Attributed to a person or document; not independently established.
|Estimate |Derived by a stated method, with uncertainty and limitations.
|Illustrative |Invented to explore behaviour; not evidence about an organisation.
|Missing |Required information is unavailable. Leave the value blank, not zero.
|===

Separate historical accounting results, current cash, contractual commitments,
service promises and prospective scenarios. Reconcile totals without converting
one category into another. Preserve contradictory evidence and revisions.

For each dependency record its direction, lag and basis: an accounting identity,
a mechanical rule, an empirical association or a causal hypothesis. An event map
does not establish causation. Shared drivers and feedback may require joint
scenarios; independently varying every input can create impossible cases.

Maintain an assumption register beside the model. A changed assumption should
identify the results and decisions that need review. Report ranges only where
their interpretation is specified; a stress scenario is not a probability.
