= Organisational design

Reusable work on roles, workload, participation, service delivery and change.
Model what people can sustainably do, not just what an organisational chart
assigns to them. Distinguish nominal headcount from available skilled capacity,
and paid hours from volunteer contributions.

For each proposed change, identify affected roles, dependencies, transition
costs, implementation time, decision authority and consultation requirements.
Legal and employment constraints require jurisdiction- and date-specific
verification; no particular legal conclusion is supplied here.

Service quality, entitlements, trust and participation belong in the decision
record. Avoid assuming that reducing staffing or suppressing demand eliminates
the underlying work or obligation. This directory is a research area, not a
recommendation to restructure any particular organisation.
