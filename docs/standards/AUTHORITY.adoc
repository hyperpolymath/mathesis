= Mathesis authority routing: policy, proofs, and evidence

Mathesis is a discovery and proof-orientation system, not a second estate standards authority. This directory is an index of the standards that govern its work, not a copied policy tree.

== Authority by responsibility

* *Estate-wide proof and verification policy*: https://github.com/metadatastician/standards/blob/main/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc[metadatastician/standards — PROOF-POLICY]. This owns the permitted trust classes, proof languages, and requirements for proof claims. Follow the standards repo's own versioning and pinned revision when verifying a historical result.
* *Estate-wide test policy*: https://github.com/metadatastician/standards/tree/main/3-practice/testing-and-benchmarking[testing and benchmarking] and https://github.com/metadatastician/standards/blob/main/3-practice/ZIGZAG-TESTING.adoc[Zigzag testing].
* *Repository-local mathematical architecture*: link:../atlas/README.adoc[Mathesis atlas], link:../catalogue/catalogue.adoc[existing proof census], and link:../../README.adoc[Mathesis README]. Mathesis may propose proof-index schemas and navigation views; a proposed ranking never becomes estate policy by appearing here.
* *Individual theorem truth and proof obligations*: the theorem's home repository, its exact source revision and its kernel-check receipts. A census row, index, file count, or interpreted dependency is not a proof.
* *Implementation assurance*: https://github.com/hyperpolymath/proven-tests-and-benches[proven-tests-and-benches] owns reusable test evidence and fixture doctrine; https://github.com/hyperpolymath/cicd-suite[cicd-suite] owns executable assurance gates. The status of a CI job is not automatically a theorem verification result.

== Distinguish proposals from decisions

The root link:../proposals/estate-mathematical-architecture.adoc[estate-mathematical-architecture] is an *unratified architecture brief*, preserved for provenance. In particular, its proposed proof grades, indexing/storage design, and implementation stages are not replacements for the canonical PROOF-POLICY or for the current census implementation.

The generic RSR architecture files under `docs/` reflect template lineage and should not be treated as independent Mathesis standards. If local documentation and estate policy disagree, record the conflict, the source revisions, and the required decision; do not silently make either one authoritative.

== Evidence contract

A discoverable proof record should eventually state: theorem identity and meaning; home repository and commit; checker and version; invocation and safe-mode options; assumptions and trusted base; completed, failed, skipped or unavailable verification status; source receipts; and whether a purported cross-system witness is independently checked or merely proposed. Never infer missing fields from a green badge.

A2ML was retired for new estate-wide declarations in favour of DEED and current canonical formats. Historical .a2ml records may remain as archival evidence, not as an excuse to create new competing canonical files.

This document is a routing guide, not a modification of any upstream rule.
