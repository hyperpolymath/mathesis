= The theory shelf

The theory shelf holds the theorems whose home is Mathesis: theory that an
estate rule, vocabulary or interface depends on, and that no single system
owns. The other two shelves are the atlas (`docs/atlas/`) and the applied
systems (`applied-systems/`).

== The admission rule

A theorem enters the shelf only when all three conditions hold:

. *It is theory* by the test of
  https://github.com/metadatastician/standards/blob/main/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc[PROOF-POLICY]
  §1. A statement that names a type, function or wire format of one
  repository is engineering, and belongs to that repository. A statement
  that would be true of any implementation is theory.
. *No single system owns it.* A theorem about one system's own behaviour
  lives in that system's repository.
. *An estate rule, vocabulary or interface depends on it,* and the record
  names that dependant.

A theorem that meets the rule is proved in Agda and re-checked in Rocq and
Lean (PROOF-POLICY §1 and §2), with a receipt in `verification/receipts/`.

== Where the records are

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Path |Contents

|`admissions.jsonl`
|One authored admission record for each theorem: why it is theory, why no
single system owns it, and the dependant (repository, path and rule). It
holds no grade.

|`generated/index.adoc`
|The theorem index, generated by `scripts/registry/claims-registry.mjs` from
the admission records, the claim pins and the receipt. Never edit it by hand.

|`../../verification/proofs/`
|The Agda, Rocq and Lean sources.

|`../../verification/receipts/theory_praxis.deed`
|The proof receipt of Mathesis (PROOF-POLICY §8.5.1).
|===

`just registry-check` (part of `just check`) fails when the index is stale,
when a home theorem has no admission record, or when an admission names no
dependant, a non-public dependant repository, or a theorem with no claim
pin.

== Admitting a theorem

. State the theorem, prove it in Agda, and re-prove it in Rocq and Lean.
. Add each leg to the receipt, from observed checker runs.
. Pin the claim in `docs/atlas/claims/claims.jsonl`.
. Add its admission record to `admissions.jsonl`.
. Run `bun scripts/registry/claims-registry.mjs build`, then `just check`.

The token-lattice theorems are the next candidates (ruling D-10). Their
statements are in `docs/proposals/mathesis-research-programme.adoc`.
